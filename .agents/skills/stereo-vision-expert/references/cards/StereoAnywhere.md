# Stereo Anywhere block card

### 0. Meta
- Title: Stereo Anywhere: Robust Zero-Shot Deep Stereo Matching Even Where Either Stereo or Mono Fail. Luca Bartolomei, Fabio Tosi, Matteo Poggi, Stefano Mattoccia (Univ. Bologna). CVPR 2025 (arXiv 2412.04472v2, 7 May 2025). 32 pages (main 8 + refs + supplement pp.16-32; read fully).
- PDF: paper/reference_papers/fusion/StereoAnywhere_Bartolomei_CVPR2025.pdf. Code/project: https://stereoanywhere.github.io/ (p.1).
- Domain: general; strongest on indoor / non-Lambertian (mirrors, glass). Datasets: trained ONLY on Scene Flow (~39k synthetic pairs, p.6); evaluated zero-shot on Middlebury 2014 (half res) & 2021, ETH3D, KITTI 2012/2015, Booster (quarter res), LayeredFlow (eighth res), new MonoTrap (26 scenes, RealSense L515 LiDAR GT); fine-tuned only for the Booster online benchmark rows.

### 1. Problem & failure modes targeted
- Stereo: limited generalization, textureless regions, occlusions, non-Lambertian surfaces (mirrors/glass) because training data lack them (Sec. 1).
- Mono VFM: scale ambiguity, perspective-illusion failures (MonoTrap). Goal: network that follows whichever cue is reliable.
- Training-data gap: SceneFlow does not contain situations where mono is needed, so a naive dual-branch net would ignore the mono branch -> volume augmentations (Sec. 3.2, 7.3).

### 2. Pipeline by stage (RAFT-Stereo codebase)
- 2a Feature extraction: image encoder (RAFT-Stereo, pretrained weights) on L/R -> F_L,F_R in R^{D x H/4 x W/4}; this encoder is FROZEN during training (p.4). Context encoder with identical architecture but its INPUT is the monocular depth map M_L (not the RGB), trainable ("optimized to extract meaningful features from these depth maps"). D (channels): not stated.
- 2b Prior branch: frozen Depth Anything V2 Large (HyperSim-trained metric-free relative depth; weights from authors) run on BOTH images -> M_L, M_R in R^{1xHxW}. VFM-agnostic: DepthPro, MoGe, Lotus also tested (supp. 8.1).
- 2c Cost volumes: two correlation volumes at 1/4: (i) stereo V_S in R^{H/4 x W/4 x W/4} = dot product of F_L,F_R (Eq. 1); (ii) monocular V_M = dot product of surface NORMAL maps (3-ch) computed from M downsampled to 1/4: nabla* = [-d(lambda M)/dx, -d(lambda M)/dy, 1] normalized, lambda = (1/10)(W/4) for scale-invariance (supp. Eq. 8). V_M is masked by N=8 relative-depth bins: ({V_M}^n)_ijk = M_L^n(i,j) * M_R^n(i,k) * V_M, M^n(i,j)=1 iff n/N <= M(i,j) < (n+1)/N, i.e. matches allowed only between pixels in the same relative-depth bin (Eqs. 3, 9). Normals are used because depth maps of L and R can be mutually inconsistent and because they are scale/shift-invariant.
- 2d Aggregation: only for the mono volume: 3D conv regularizer phi_A (architecture of IGEV 3D-reg. [116] with permuted dims, N=8 bin volumes in -> V'_M in R^{F x H/4 x W/4 x W/4}) plus an adapted CoEx volume excitation using BOTH views' shallow 2D features of M_L, M_R (supp. Eq. 10: V' = sigmoid(f_L) * sigmoid(f_R) * V). Two shallow 3D conv heads: phi_D -> V_M^D (disparity volume), phi_C -> V_M^C (confidence volume). Stereo volume V_S gets NO aggregation. F, kernel sizes: not stated.
- 2e Disparity computation (coarse, from mono branch): soft-argmax over the W/4 axis of V_M^D: D^_L = j - softargmax_L(V_M^D) (Eq. 4), right disparity from the first W axis (supp. Eqs. 11-12). Used only to scale the mono depth and to supervise, plus inside truncation.
- 2f Iterative update: RAFT multi-GRU. D1 = M^_L (scaled mono) initialization; each iteration has TWO lookups (Fig. 2 (7)): G_S from the stereo pyramid, G_M from V_M^D; both pass through the same two-layer encoder, concatenated with encoded current disparity, 2D conv, ConvGRU; context-from-mono features condition the GRU. 12 iterations train, 32 test. Pyramid levels/radius: inherited from RAFT-Stereo, not restated.
- 2g Upsampling: RAFT convex upsampling.
- 2h FUSION POINTS (all mono<->stereo; semantic: none):
  1. Context level: GRU context encoder takes mono depth M_L as input (retrained; ablation C).
  2. Cost volume (second volume): normal-based mono correlation volume with depth-bin masking, aggregated by 3D conv + view-conditioned excitation; its lookup G_M fed to the GRU every iteration alongside stereo lookup G_S (explicit dual-lookup).
  3. Init/scale: differentiable weighted least-squares scale s^ and shift t^ aligning M_L and M_R jointly to coarse disparities D^_L, D^_R, weighted by confidence C^ (Eq. 6/16): min_{s,t} sum_{L,R} || sqrt(C^) .* [(sM + t) - D^] ||_F; M^ = s^M + t^ (Eq. 7). A single GLOBAL scale/shift per image (both views jointly for L-R consistency), NOT per-pixel; per-pixel mismatch is left to the GRU.
  4. Stereo cost volume truncation (mono -> stereo, for mirrors): truncate mask T = [(M^_L > D^_L) AND C_M] OR [C_M AND NOT C^_L] built with fuzzy operators (AND=a*b, OR=a+b-ab, NOT=1-a, GREATER=sigmoid(a-b), supp. Eqs. 17-18; C_M = softLRC_L(M^_L, M^_R)); where T' > T_m = 0.98, V_S is multiplied by V_T, a sigmoid curve centred at the correlation index of the scaled mono disparity: V_T = (1-T') + T'[sigma(j - M^_L - k)(1-G) + G], G = 0.9 (Eq. 19). Rationale: on a mirror stereo sees "through" it (farther than the surface), so mono-nearer-than-stereo AND mono-confident -> suppress stereo hypotheses behind the mono surface. Hand-crafted, not learned (thresholds, G).
  5. Loss coupling: coarse outputs and confidences supervised (Sec. 5/7.5).
- CONFIDENCE MECHANISMS (explicit, three layers):
  a. Entropy confidence of the mono volume: C^_L(i,j) = 1 + [sum_d softmax(V_M^C)_ijd * log2 softmax(V_M^C)_ijd] / log2(W/4) in [0,1] (Eq. 5/13), i.e. 1 - normalized Shannon entropy of the cost curve; monomodal curve = reliable. Right map analogously over the first W axis (Eq. 14). Learned: V_M^C comes from its own conv head phi_C and C^ is supervised with BCE against C_gt = softplus-ratio softLRC of |D^ - D_gt| (Eqs. 24-25; target = soft correctness of the coarse disparity at threshold T_LRC=1 px).
  b. Occlusion masking via softLRC: softLRC(D,D_R) = log(1+exp(T_LRC - |D_L - W_L(D_L, D_R)|)) / log(1+exp(T_LRC)), T_LRC = 1 (Eq. 15); multiplied into C^ so occluded pixels get low confidence before the scale fit.
  c. Implicit arbitration in the GRU between stereo lookup G_S and mono lookup G_M, shaped by training-time volume augmentations (below). The net outputs no final confidence map.
- Volume augmentations (training only; one applied at a time, to V_M^D or V_S, only on a random section chosen via a mask M^n_L): Volume Rolling (shift matching peak to a random position along the last W axis - emulates mirror/glass false peak), Volume Noising (uniform [0,1) noise - emulates textureless/repetition), Volume Zeroing (Gaussian-like curve centered at disparity zero - emulates occlusion); plus Perfect Monocular augmentation: replace the VFM depth with the ground truth normalized to [0,1] (noise-free mono so the mono branch gains importance). Purpose stated: "Stereo Anywhere cannot properly learn when to choose stereo or mono information from [SceneFlow] alone" (p.5); "Without any augmentation of this kind, the stereo network would simply overlook the additional information from the monocular branch" (supp. 7.3).
- Real-time variant: none. Cost: stereo backbone ~50% slower than RAFT-Stereo; DAv2 ~0.03 s per image; at 256x256: total 0.18 s / 0.76 GB (DAv2 0.03 s/0.57 GB + stereo 0.15 s/0.18 GB) vs RAFT-Stereo 0.10 s / 0.17 GB; 512x512: 0.24 s / 1.34 GB vs 0.14 s / 0.66 GB; 1024x1024: 0.63 s / 6.31 GB vs 0.36 s / 2.63 GB (Tab. 7, single A100, with DAv2).

### 4. Interactions & dependencies
- Mono branch is useless (or harmful, see B on Middlebury +3.81) unless the context net is re-trained on mono depth and a mono volume with scale alignment is added; the full gain needs the volume augmentations, otherwise the GRU ignores the mono branch (authors' statement, supp. 7.3).
- Scaling quality depends on the confidence maps (entropy + softLRC), which depend on the mono volume's own matching ability; masks by depth bins reduce ambiguity because normals are texture-less (matching "not ambiguous only in edges", p.4).
- Truncation depends on scaled mono M^, the coarse stereo-from-mono D^, and mono confidence C_M: mono must be good for truncation to be valid; thresholds T_m=0.98, G=0.9 are hand-set.
- Frozen RAFT-Stereo image encoder and starting from the Scene Flow RAFT-Stereo checkpoint: stereo cue is "as in RAFT"; only the extra branch + GRU adapt.
- VFM quality matters differently per dataset (see Tab. 5/6); DAv2 best for non-Lambertian, MoGe for outdoor.
- Mono and stereo branch costs: dual cost volume doubles memory at 1 Mpx (2.63 -> 5.73 GB for the stereo network, Tab. 7).

### 5. Losses (supp. 7.5)
- L = L_A + L_B + L_C + L_D + L_E + L_F + L_G (Eq. 26; all weights 1 except the normal term psi=10).
- L_A: RAFT sequence loss sum_l gamma^{L-l} ||D^l - D_Lgt||_1 (Eq. 20; gamma not restated, RAFT default 0.9 not stated here).
- L_B, L_C: ||D^_L - D_Lgt||_1 + psi ||1 - nabla_L . nabla^_L||_1 for left/right coarse mono-volume disparities (L1 + normal-consistency, psi=10) (Eqs. 21-22; right-side formula as printed uses nabla_L, likely a typo).
- L_D, L_E: L1 on scaled mono M^_L, M^_R (Eq. 23).
- L_F, L_G: BCE(C^, C_gt) with C_gt = softplus-ratio of |D^ - D_gt| (soft correctness at 1 px) (Eqs. 24-25).

### 6. Training recipe
- Single A100, starting from the RAFT-Stereo Scene Flow checkpoint; 3 epochs on Scene Flow; LR 1e-4; AdamW; batch 2; random 320x640 crops; standard RAFT color/spatial augmentation; 12 GRU iterations train, 32 test. Frozen: image feature encoder (RAFT-Stereo), DAv2-Large. Trainable: context encoder (on mono depth), phi_A / phi_D / phi_C, update operator (GRU etc.). Volume augmentations + perfect-mono substitution during training. Booster-FT: details not stated beyond "fine-tuned".

### 7. Results
- See Sec. 3 table (zero-shot Scene Flow-only unless marked). Params: not stated anywhere. Runtime/memory at 256/512/1024 square (Tab. 7, A100): 0.18/0.24/0.63 s total with DAv2, 0.76/1.34/6.31 GB; RAFT-Stereo 0.10/0.14/0.36 s, 0.17/0.66/2.63 GB. Scene Flow in-domain EPE is NOT reported.

### 8. Negative results & limitations
- Authors: truncation cannot fully fix mirrors (supp. 8.2); DepthPro fails on Middlebury 2021 scenes (supp. 8.1); (B) off-the-shelf mono context hurts Middlebury (+3.81 bad>2).
- No ablation of the main sub-components (listed in the table); (D) and (E) bundle 3-4 changes each.
- No latency/real-time analysis beyond A100 numbers; ~2x memory at 1 Mpx; needs two VFM passes (L and R); not deployable on 4 GB / Jetson without a tiny VFM.
- No Scene Flow in-domain number; all gains zero-shot or fine-tuned Booster, so in-domain accuracy cost (if any) is unknown.
- Compared stereo baselines are off-the-shelf SceneFlow checkpoints (fair for zero-shot), but only RAFT-Stereo shares the data pipeline; some baselines (ELFNet, PSMNet) are weak, inflating the table.
- Mono prior reliance: on ETH3D avg-px gain is tiny (0.24 vs 0.25); gains concentrate on Occ, Booster, and Middlebury.
- Errors found in summaries/fusion/StereoAnywhere.md: (1) states normals are used because they are "scale-invariant ... naturally compatible with absolute disparity" - paper's reasons are non-ambiguity/consistency between L and R mono maps and scale-invariance via lambda, OK, but the summary omits the 3D hourglass aggregation phi_A + CoEx excitation, which is more than "spatial gradients + dot products, no heavy processing" (efficiency claim is wrong: the mono branch includes a 3D conv regularizer on an H/4 x W/4 x W/4 volume); (2) omits the volume augmentations, the paper's key training mechanism (and the perfect-mono augmentation); (3) says "global scale/shift (single solve) is cheap ... reducing iterations" - the paper uses 12 train / 32 test iterations like RAFT; no reduced-iteration claim; (4) "frozen RAFT-Stereo feature encoder" correct, but context encoder is trained; (5) "MonoTrap ... painted checkerboards, murals" is not in the paper text (it says planar patterns creating illusions such as holes in walls/floors and simulated transparent surfaces); (6) says "Ranks 1st on Booster leaderboard (fine-tuned)" - paper says first at quarter resolution only; (7) says confidence C^ from "entropy of the mono correlation volume" - right, but omits that it comes from a separate learned head phi_C and softLRC occlusion masking; (8) "volume truncation virtually free" - it is a handful of elementwise ops on H/4xW/4xW/4, but the fuzzy mask needs both D^ and M^ (3D conv outputs), so it is not free.

### 9. Relevance to OUR model
This is the paper with the most explicit confidence-gating machinery, so it is the closest analogue of SemanticCostGate. Our fusion head is trained on a frozen A09 with VKITTI in-domain data; their key lesson is that a gate learns to ignore the extra branch unless trained with corrupted stereo hypotheses.
- Portable 1: entropy-based confidence of the stereo cost curve: C = 1 + sum p log2 p / log2(K) over our K disparity candidates/tile hypotheses (p = softmax of candidate scores). Free (prior-agnostic), differentiable, in [0,1], computed on the A09 candidate costs. Insertion: as an input channel (and/or multiplicative modulator) to SemanticCostGate, so semantics override only where the stereo curve is multimodal. Expected benefit: sharper where-to-trust-semantics than class alone; likely helps in occlusion/textureless (where SA gains concentrate: Occ columns). Cost: negligible. Risk: A09 cost curves from tiles/planes may already be peaky; calibrate against actual error (SA trains it with BCE vs soft correctness C_gt, which we can copy: BCE(C, softLRC-style |d-d_gt|) as an auxiliary loss on the gate input, a new loss for our D/E series).
- Portable 2: softLRC occlusion mask (needs right-view disparity; we have stereo pair, but A09 may not output right disparity; cost = a second pass or symmetric head). Moderate cost; benefit mostly for occluded regions where semantics matter.
- Portable 3 (analogue of depth-bin masking): SA restricts matching to same-depth-bin pixels via M_L^n(i,j) M_R^n(i,k). Categorical equivalent: class-consistency mask on the cost volume/candidates: down-weight candidates whose right-view pixel has a different semantic class than the left pixel (A09's shared trunk gives class logits for both views for free). Directly implementable as a soft multiplicative gate on candidates, restricted to non-ambiguous (large) classes. This is not "already done" in our D/E series (D1 gates by semantics but not by L-R class consistency, as far as the AGENTS.md describes). Risk: semantic errors near boundaries kill the true match; use soft gating and learned temperature.
- Portable 4: volume augmentations (rolling a peak to a wrong bin, noise, zeroing) applied to the stereo candidate volume during gate training so that the semantic branch must supply the right answer; plus an "oracle prior" substitution (their perfect monocular = GT normalized; ours = GT label map / teacher labels with some probability). This directly attacks the "gate ignores semantics" failure and could amplify our existing -0.24 px in-domain and KITTI semantic gains; cheap (training only). Risk: synthetic corruption may not match KITTI failure modes; keep the equal-capacity no-semantics control with identical augmentation to attribute gains.
- NOT transferable: normals-from-depth volume, differentiable scale/shift (needs a metric-free continuous prior), truncation by mono-depth ordering (a categorical "mirror/glass" class could drive a truncation analogue, but our 14 VKITTI classes have no mirrors; irrelevant for driving), dual full cost volumes with 3D hourglass (4 GB budget). Runtime of the paper (0.24 s at 512x512 on A100) is not real-time.
- Evidence caveat: all SA gains are with a dense continuous VFM prior; none of the blocks was ablated individually, so the attribution of gains to confidence vs volume vs context vs augmentation is unknown. Do not cite as evidence for categorical priors.

### 10. Key quotes/equations worth citing
- "Stereo Anywhere cannot properly learn when to choose stereo or mono information from [Scene Flow] alone. Hence, we propose three volume augmentations and a monocular augmentation" (p.5).
- Entropy confidence: C^_L = 1 + [sum_d softmax(V_M^C)_ijd log2 softmax(V_M^C)_ijd] / log2(W/4) (Eq. 5, p.4); "clear monomodal-like cost curves - those with low entropy - are reliable".
- Weighted LS: min_{s,t} sum_{L,R} ||sqrt(C^) .* [(sM+t) - D^]||_F (Eq. 6, p.4).
- Truncation condition (p.5) and Eq. 19: V_T = (1-T') + T'[sigma(j - M^_L - k)(1-G) + G], G=0.9 (supp. p.18).
- Tab. 1 ablation (p.6): (A) 17.84 -> (E) 9.01 Booster bad>2.
