# Training recipes distilled from 35 papers

Every paper number carries its Tab./Eq./p. ref from the verified cards. "not stated" means the card says so; do not fill it in.
Nearly all paper ablations are single-seed on tiny val sets (KITTI 40 imgs, Middlebury 15 pairs) with no variance: deltas below ~0.05 px EPE are not evidence. Almost none have an equal-capacity no-semantics control.

Cross-cutting warnings
- Label IDs are categorical: |phi(sem)| on class indices (SSPCV first order, SGNet second order) depends on label numbering. Build a one-hot boundary indicator (class change within a neighbourhood) instead; mask classes whose semantic edges are not depth edges (road, sidewalk, vegetation, terrain; SGNet Fig. 4).
- Effect sizes are tiny and uncontrolled: SGNet loss module -0.0005 px; SCG -0.01 px; SSPCV -0.03 px; LRSC -0.012 px; SegStereo 0.00-0.03 px in supervised fine-tune. None has an equal-capacity no-semantics control, seeds, or an out-of-domain test; several tune loss weights on the reporting split (SGNet, S3M-Net, TiCoSS).
- Our own G/H series found edge-targeted heads did not move overall EPE; expect the same class of small gains from boundary losses.

## Loss ladder (project-tuned)

Weights = our Driving-200 tuning in `experiments/a05_clean/losses.py:modal_loss` (ported from the modal training script `train_arch_sceneflow.py`); re-tune per dataset and ablate each step for dEPE/dbad1.

1. `L1(full)` — in the code this is plain masked L1 (`ms_l1`/inline abs), not smooth-L1; sL1 is the paper default (CoEx, BGNet, LightStereo). Plateaus ~6 px on Driving-200.
2. `+ 0.5·L1(1/2) + 0.3·L1(1/4)` — computed by avg-pooling the full-res prediction and GT (heads emit only full-res). Biggest single jump on Driving-200; teaches coarse structure. This is our own port, not the RTS2Net 1/4,1/2,1 or SGNet 0.5/0.7/1.0 schedules (those weight separate stage outputs, the coarse stage heavier or lighter in the opposite direction).
3. `+ 0.5·grad_consistency` — disparity gradients match GT gradients; sharpens edges without a refinement net. Code weight is 0.5 (line 116); the file docstring says 0.2, so the docstring is stale. No paper card uses this term.
4. `+ 0.2·threshold_hinge + 0.2·d1_hinge` — quadratic penalties above 0.5/1/2/3 px and the D1 region; pulls bad-1/bad-3/D1. Same family as HITNet's confidence/robust truncations, not a port of them.
5. `+ 0.02·edge_smooth` — image-weighted smoothness; keep tiny. Papers use smaller (DispSegNet g3=0.005 with segment-aware features) or none; naive smoothness hurts small classes (DispSegNet Tab. III).
6. With semantics: boundary loss. SSPCV weight 0.1 first-order unmasked; SGNet 0.5 second-order masked (m_b, lambda=3). Use one-hot boundary indicators, see warnings above.
7. Iterative heads: L1-seq gamma=0.9 (GGEV, FoundationStereo, DEFOM, PromptStereo, MonSter, D-FUSE, LAS2-H). Not used in our A05 ladder; unablated in the papers.
8. Deep-supervision staging: SGNet 0.5/0.7/1.0 (disp3 unsupervised), SemStereo 1/0.6/0.5/0.3, RTS2Net 1/4,1/2,1.

## Augmentation

Epipolar-safe set that appears in cards (identically applied vertical geometry to both views, no independent L/R warps):
- Asymmetric chromatic / brightness-contrast: BGNet (50 % each, with y-disparity aug, blur, scale zoom); HITNet symmetric [0.8,1.2], asymmetric [0.95,1.05]; ESMStereo brightness/contrast/saturation only; GGEV asymmetric chromatic + spatial; LightStereo colour jitter, random erase, random scale for generalisation runs (whether used in SF training ambiguous); USAM-Net 10 % jitter.
- y-offset: HITNet +-2 px, generated at H/64 x W/64 and bilinearly upsampled; BGNet y-disparity aug.
- HITNet right-patch transplant: replace a rectangle (50x50 to 180x250) in the RIGHT image with a crop from elsewhere in the right image; plus Middlebury colour normalisation (AdaStereo) and Gaussian noise var in [0,5] once per image (HITNet p.13).
- LAS2 perturbation (student only, Stage 2): large colour jitter, optional 5x5 blur sigma [0.1,2], gamma [0.7,1.5], symmetric or asymmetric with small probability (LAS2 Sec. IV-B).
- Random crop everywhere; RAFT-Stereo augmentation recipe in FoundationStereo, PromptStereo, StereoAnywhere.
- Never: vertical flip, independent left/right warps, anything breaking horizontal epipolar lines.
- Stage-1 "NO augmentation" is deliberate in LiteAnyStereo/LAS2 (1.8M synthetic) and SemStereo.
- Cardless / unverified: scale 0.9-1.15 with disparity rescaling (no card gives this range; SegStereo resizes 0.5-2.0 with a Caffe pipeline, handling of disparity not stated).

Volume / prior augmentations (StereoAnywhere, supp. 7.3; training only, one at a time on a random mask section of V_M^D or V_S):
- Rolling: shift matching peak to a random position along the disparity axis (mimics mirror/glass false peak). Noising: uniform [0,1) noise (textureless/repetition). Zeroing: Gaussian-like curve at disparity 0 (occlusion). Perfect-mono: replace the VFM depth with GT normalised to [0,1] (oracle prior).
- Authors: without these the GRU cannot learn when to trust stereo vs mono (p.5). Effect: (D)->(E) Booster bad>2 -2.32 pt, Middlebury All -0.71 pt, but (E) also adds truncation; each augmentation is never ablated on its own (StereoAnywhere Tab. 1).

Never ablated alone: HITNet patch transplant, y-offset, colour-transfer; LightStereo generalisation augmentations; LAS2 perturbations vs plain colour aug beyond the Stage-2 rows above; all StereoAnywhere volume augmentations; CoEx must train (not only test) with top-k: k=48 train / k=2 test 0.7782 vs 0.7426 matched (CoEx Full, SF-only, 10 ep, CoEx table).

## Curricula, pretraining, pseudo-labels

- Standard: SceneFlow -> KITTI/Middlebury/ETH3D finetune. HITNet: FlyingThings3D only (adding Driving/Monkaa hurt, p.12) and trains KITTI from scratch on 394 imgs with heavy aug.
- Real-domain semantic pretraining: RTS2Net (c=1 AnyNet-like): SF 10 ep + KITTI 300 ep EPE 1.24 / D1 6.47; SF 40 + KITTI 800 1.18 / 6.28; Cityscapes 60 coarse -> 75 fine + KITTI 800 1.14 / 5.75 (RTS2Net Tab. I). Cityscapes disparity GT is SGM (noisy), so the gain is not purely semantic. SegStereo: Cityscapes (SGM) 90k -> Cityscapes extra + FT3D 500k with seg net fixed and softmax loss off -> KITTI 90k (SegStereo Tab. 2).
- LiteAnyStereo 3-stage: S1 synthetic 1.8M no aug -> S2 fixed-teacher feature self-distillation with perturbed student -> S3 0.5M unlabeled real pairs with FoundationStereo pseudo labels. ## Debugging signatures

Project (our runs):
- Train spiky / val smooth -> a few unmatchable pairs dominate random crops. Score all pairs, rank by EPE, inspect brightness + valid fraction. (A04b: 2/200 black tunnel frames.)
- Crop EPE collapses / held-out stalls -> overfit on small budget; iterative heads resisted better than wide aggregation heads (A04: RAFT 5.87 vs M 6.52).
- Edges melted -> bilinear upsampling; try bilateral slicing, plane-tile upsampling (HITNet "no slant", bilinear upsampling: SF EPE 0.548 vs 0.529, Tab. 6), or grad-consistency loss. Our G/H screens: RGB-guided residual, learned convex upsampling, stereo-warp correction did not visibly sharpen.
- Poles/signs worse with smoothness -> segment-aware weighting (DispSegNet Tab. III: pole 11.26 -> 13.13 without seg, 7.62 with) or masked boundary loss (SGNet m_b).

Paper-backed:
- Texture-only dynamic kernels give in-domain but no zero-shot gain -> the depth prior is missing (GGEV Tab. 4, +DCA row); SCF alone can hurt KITTI zero-shot (K12 5.14 -> 4.11 with DDCA). Pointwise 1x1 fusion generalises better than 3x3.
- Unfreezing a prior encoder corrupts it: FoundationStereo Tab. 5, unfrozen ViT 3.94 vs frozen 1.97 BP-2; DPT-pyramid-only (no CNN) 6.48; DINOv2 2.46 vs DAv2-L 1.97.
- Naive prior concat / unguarded fusion hurts generalisation: D-FUSE mono context alone (ME) gives the big jump (EPE 2.11 -> 1.42), naive depth fusion adds nothing on top, and ME alone is worse than RAFT on KITTI-15 (1.18/6.18 vs 1.13/5.69, Tab. 1); replacing the stereo feature extractor by DA-V2 gives 3.26/28.73 (supp Tab. 13). StereoAnywhere: off-the-shelf mono context hurts Middlebury (+3.81 bad>2, row B). USAM-Net: SAM mask as raw extra input channels: Seg+Attn D1 10.04 vs baseline 8.57 before fine-tune (USAM-Net Tab. 3); sky masking hurt accuracy (unquantified).
- Smoothness hurts small classes without seg-awareness: DispSegNet Tab. III (above).
- Mono branch ignored by the GRU when trained on SceneFlow alone -> volume augmentations + perfect-mono substitution (StereoAnywhere p.5); the same risk applies to a semantic gate trained on clean candidate volumes. Keep an identical-augmentation no-semantics control.
- Zero-init iterative heads (RAFT) cannot be pruned to 1-2 iterations: Pip-Stereo needs a regularised (IGEV-style) init; EPE stays >2 at 1 iteration with zero init.
- Wider/more expressive fusion is not better in low-data regimes: concat/gated fusion below plain addition (S3M-Net Tab. V: addition 54.33 vs concat 48.40, SA-Gate 52.10 KITTI mIoU) while TiCoSS reports gains from gates (+5 mIoU); the two disagree and neither controls capacity (TiCoSS 385 M params).
- Foundation-prior gains are dataset-dependent: GGEV ViT-L prior lowers indoor zero-shot (Middlebury 6.53 -> 4.41, ETH3D 2.84 -> 1.69) but not KITTI (4.11 -> 4.20); in driving the S prior already saturates.
- Pseudo-label degradation: unclamped/unfiltered pseudo labels (LAS2: K12 3.17 vs 2.88); unsupervised photometric training on new data degrades both tasks (TwInS Tab. V); more unclean data hurts (Stereo4D, Xperience, LAS2 Tab. VI).
- Seg/disparity trade-off: refinement that raises disparity can lower mIoU (RTS2Net 64.21 -> 62.22, DispSegNet 47.6 -> 46.9); log both when a head touches shared features.
- Mirrors piercing the volume -> truncation at mono disparity (StereoAnywhere; evidence qualitative, Fig. 8, and bundled in row E). Dark/low-texture failures -> DA/DINO priors as guidance only (AIO roles), not as the sole feature extractor.
