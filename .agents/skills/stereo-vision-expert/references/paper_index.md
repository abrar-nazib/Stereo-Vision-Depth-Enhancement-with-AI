# Paper index (38 verified cards)

Cards: `cards/<Name>.md` (sections 1-8 hold mechanism, ablation table, results, negatives). PDF paths are repo-relative. Venues are as verified inside each card; "arXiv only" = the PDF names no venue.
Grade rubric: **A** capacity-matched control or multi-seed/variance, held-out data; **B** component ablations on held-out/val, single run, no capacity control; **C** ablations on test split / tuned on test, key claim unablated, or confounded; **D** negative/cautionary result or draft-quality. Ref format: Tab. N = table in that paper. "In-domain" = trained/fine-tuned on the evaluated dataset family; "zero-shot" = synthetic-only training, held-out real data.

## Known file/metadata issues

- Card count: 38 cards (16 semantic-stereo, 10 prior-fusion, 11 lightweight, 1 deployment [VPEngine, stored in lightweight/]). SAOF, SIGLNet, SSPGNet added after the citation search; SSPGNet sits under semantic_stereo/ on disk but is a prior-fusion paper.
- `MonSter_Cheng_CVPR2025.pdf` actually contains MonSter++ (arXiv 2501.08643v2, 25 Sep 2025); original MonSter is CVPR 2025 but all numbers in the card are MonSter++. Repo summary's "RT-MonSter ~20M params" is not in the PDF.
- `USAM-Net_Sankaranarayanan_arXiv2025.pdf`: PDF authors are Dayo and Naval Jr. (arXiv 2503.14950v1); "Sankaranarayanan" in filename and repo summary is wrong.
- Venue only arXiv in the PDF: PromptStereo (CVPR 2026 appears only in the filename), ViTAS (arXiv 2404.06261v1, IEEE layout, no journal), TwInS (arXiv Feb 2026, unpublished), JointTreeEncoders (arXiv 2609.13232), SENSE, USAM-Net, LiteAnyStereoV2, ESMStereo ("JImaging2026" in filename only), VPEngine ("RAP2026" in filename only), MonSter(++) v2.
- Venue not in PDF but inferred: FoundationStereo (CVPR 2025 from PromptStereo's reference list); LiteAnyStereo V1 (V2 card says CVPR 2026; PDF is arXiv v2).
- Supplement/appendix referenced but absent from our PDF: TiCoSS, TwInS, Pip-Stereo (appendix), LiteAnyStereo, SegStereo (card: "missing supplement"). RTS2Net: supplement is only a YouTube video.
- Cards that state the PDF has no supplement/appendix (nothing further to find): DTPnet, DispSegNet, LightStereo, AIO-Stereo, SDBF-Net, S3Net, S3M-Net, SemStereo, SGNet, USAM-Net, ViTAS, SSPCV-Net, LiteAnyStereoV2, ESMStereo, JointTreeEncoders.
- PhraseStereo: PDF is on disk (`paper/reference_papers/semantic_stereo/PhraseStereo_Campagnolo_ICCVW2025.pdf`) but has no card; it is only skimmed in the last section of the SENSE card. SENSE training stereo is synthetic (GenStereo from DAv2 depth) per that section.
- S2Net: `paper/reference_papers/summaries/semantic_stereo/S2Net.md` exists but no PDF (closed access, abstract only). Its numbers appear only as copied comparators in S3Net, SemStereo, RTS2Net cards.
- Repo summaries with card-verified errors are catalogued in `summary_errata.md` (e.g. SSPCV-Net 0.87 EPE is Scene Flow not KITTI; SegStereo semantic net is frozen, not jointly trained; BGNet code URL wrong).
- Internal inconsistencies flagged in cards: ESMStereo arXiv date; DEFOM param-text vs table; Pip-Stereo speed ratios (22x claimed, 17x from table); LiteAnyStereo "4K at 21 ms" vs 33 GMACs; DTPnet "lowest EPE" vs Tab. VII.
